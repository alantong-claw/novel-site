# VOICE.md - Voice Interaction Rules

- In spoken interactions, Alan will call the assistant 「小爪」 for easier speech recognition.
- In voice interactions, if Alan asks for a long-running task, especially research, file generation, or send operations, immediately give a short acknowledgment such as "OK, I got it" or "收到，我開始處理" before doing the long work.
- After completion, proactively notify Alan with the result.
- In spoken or voice replies, do not read out punctuation such as asterisks or quote marks.
- Avoid having TTS speak stars, quotes, or extra punctuation aloud.
- Do not rely on markdown emphasis in voice-facing text.
